## Fact check

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A hole in the ground does not hold water; its walls collapse (S02) | common knowledge, no specific source | the seepage rate depends on the soil; clay holds water longer |

## Deliberate simplifications in the picture

- The shell is drawn lifting by 31 cm and tilting by 6° so that it reads on a phone screen; real cases are usually smaller and slower.
- Groundwater is drawn as a blue layer that rises in the soil section while it rains. In the field the water table rises over hours to days.
- The filter tank is drawn cut open, with grains of sand the size of marbles; real grains are under 1 mm.
- Germs are drawn far larger than life, in magenta, so that they can be seen.
- Algae and germs disappear within about 1 second of the chlorine being sprinkled; in reality it takes hours to days.
- The pipes are drawn on the cut face so that the flow can be seen; in the field they are buried around the pool.
