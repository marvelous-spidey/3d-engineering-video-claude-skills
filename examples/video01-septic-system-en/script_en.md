## Deliberate simplifications

- The well sits close to the pit to make the risk visible; real setback distances are set by state and local codes.
- One trench is shown; real drain fields have several trenches fed through a distribution box.
- Pipes, solids and bacteria are drawn much larger than life so they read on a phone screen.
- "Fills up in days" assumes a tank of about 1,000 gallons and a household using a few hundred gallons a day.
